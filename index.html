About Us

PT Ekasanti Jayamulia is an Indonesian Migrant Workers Placement Company (P3MI) established in 1991 and licensed by the Minister of Manpower to provide services for the placement of Indonesian migrant workers abroad.

Why Us?

  • Over 30 Years of Industry Experience
  • Safe, Legal, and Government-Regulated Placement
  • Global Workforce Placement Network
  • Commitment to Quality and Worker Welfare
  • End-to-End Worker Protection, from Departure to Return
