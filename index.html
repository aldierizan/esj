About Us

Committed to People,
Driven by Opportunities

PT Ekasanti Jayamulia is an Indonesian Migrant Workers Placement Company (P3MI) established in 1991 and licensed by the Minister of Manpower to provide services for the placement of Indonesian migrant workers abroad.

With more than three decades of experience, we have placed thousands of Indonesian workers across formal and informal sectors in Taiwan, Malaysia, Hong Kong, and Singapore.

Building on this strong foundation, we continue to expand our international network, including through developing opportunities in Türkiye and Slovakia, while building long-term partnerships with employers and institutions worldwide.
